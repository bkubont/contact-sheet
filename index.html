How do I get my contacts in?

iPhone: open the Contacts app, tap Lists (top-left), press and hold All Contacts → Export. Save the .vcf to Files, then use Import here.

Android / Google: go to contacts.google.com → Export → vCard or Google CSV, then import the file here.

Your contacts are stored only on this device. Use Back up now and then to keep a copy.
