Privacy Policy

Contact Sheet · Last updated [DATE]

Contact Sheet helps you keep a list of contacts, tag them, and print them as a PDF. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Using the app without an account

If you don't sign in, everything you add or import stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Using the app with an account

If you create an account, we store:

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password, so you can sign in.
  • The contacts you add or import (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, birthdays, notes and tags), so they are available on every device you sign in on.

This information is used only to provide the app to you. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, use it for tracking, or contact the people in your contact list.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, [REGION, e.g. "in the United States"], encrypted in transit and at rest. Each account can only access its own contacts.

PDFs

PDFs are created on your device. They are only shared if you choose to share them.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual contacts at any time. To delete your account and every contact stored in it, open the menu and choose Delete my account. This is permanent and takes effect immediately.

Children

Contact Sheet is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
